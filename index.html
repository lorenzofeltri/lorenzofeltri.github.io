03 Experiments

Financial markets & education

Archived

I built a custom WordPress website focused on the Italian financial market, with in-depth content about investment certificates, ETFs, financial taxation and individual securities.

I used SEO and search intent extensively, including instrument-specific searches around ISIN codes.

Traffic ~1,000 visits / month
Ad revenue €10–15 / month

What I learned
Good content isn't necessarily a good business. The economics of the model matter as much as the quality of what you build.

04 About

My background is in electronics, but I've always been more interested in what technology makes possible than in staying inside a single discipline.

After working as a maintenance technician, I started exploring web development, WordPress, PHP, hosting and SEO. I experimented with content websites, affiliate marketing and advertising, and built a niche financial education website from scratch.

What interests me now is the whole journey from an idea to something real: building the product, figuring out how to sell it and understanding the economics behind it.

I've also spent a significant amount of time studying financial markets. It's not what I want to build my identity around, but it has strongly influenced how I think about risk, capital and incentives.
