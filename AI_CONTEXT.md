# AI Context — Lorenzo Feltri Personal Website

## Purpose

This repository contains Lorenzo Feltri's personal website.

The website is not intended to be a traditional CV, developer
portfolio, or list of skills.

It should communicate Lorenzo as a curious, technically-minded
person who explores ideas and turns interesting ones into real
products or experiments.

Core positioning:

> Curious about ideas. Serious about building them.

---

## Identity

Name: Lorenzo Feltri

Background:
- Diploma in electronics.
- Worked as a maintenance technician.
- Strong personal interest in computer science and technology.
- Basic-to-intermediate practical experience with web development.
- Experience with WordPress, PHP, hosting and SEO.
- Experience experimenting with content websites, affiliate marketing
  and advertising.
- Strong personal knowledge of financial markets.
- Currently exploring drone hardware.

---

## Personal Positioning

The central idea is:

> A technically-minded and curious person who explores problems,
> builds things and tries to understand how to make them work
> as real businesses.

The website should communicate three recurring capabilities:

### Build
Turning ideas into working products.

### Sell
Understanding demand, search intent, positioning, content and
distribution.

### Structure
Thinking about business economics, costs, incentives and
fiscal structure.

---

## Hidden Depth: Financial Markets

Financial markets are an important part of Lorenzo's knowledge,
but they should NOT be the main identity of the personal website.

Do not position Lorenzo primarily as:
- a financial expert;
- a financial advisor;
- a trader;
- an investment professional.

Financial knowledge should appear as a deeper layer that explains
how Lorenzo thinks about risk, capital, incentives and economics.

---

## Previous Experiments

### Financial markets & education website

Lorenzo previously built a custom WordPress website focused on
the Italian financial market.

Topics included:
- investment certificates;
- ETFs;
- individual securities;
- Italian taxation;
- financial education;
- broker-specific topics;
- ISIN-based SEO.

The website reached approximately 1,000 visits/month.

Advertising revenue through Ezoic was approximately €10–15/month.

No newsletter, affiliate system or information product was developed.

The project is currently offline.

Important lesson:

> Good content is not necessarily a good business.
> The economics of the model matter as much as the quality
> of what is built.

The experiment should be presented honestly and should not be
made to appear more successful than it was.

## Website Philosophy

The website should feel alive and evolve with Lorenzo.

It is not a monument to past achievements.

It should document:
- what Lorenzo is currently exploring;
- what he is building;
- experiments he has run;
- what he learned;
- ideas worth sharing.

Honesty is more important than appearing impressive.

If there are no current projects, do not invent projects simply
to fill the website.

---

## Avoid

Do NOT turn the website into:
- a generic developer portfolio;
- a traditional CV;
- a list of technologies;
- a collection of skill bars;
- a "10+ years experience" style personal brand;
- an exaggerated founder story;
- a website that makes Lorenzo appear to be doing
  ten unrelated businesses simultaneously.

Avoid unnecessary buzzwords.

Avoid making every interest into a "business".

---

## Tone

The tone should be:
- intelligent;
- curious;
- understated;
- technically competent;
- honest;
- slightly unconventional;
- concise.

Avoid:
- corporate language;
- motivational quotes;
- exaggerated claims;
- startup clichés;
- excessive self-promotion.

The website should create curiosity rather than trying to
impress the visitor.

---

## Current Website Structure

The preferred structure is:

1. Home
2. Currently exploring
3. What I do
   - Build
   - Sell
   - Structure
4. Experiments
5. About
6. Notes

A dedicated Projects section is not necessary until there are
real projects worth showing.

---

## Design Direction

Preferred visual direction:
- minimal;
- editorial;
- typography-focused;
- clean;
- lots of whitespace;
- restrained color palette;
- no unnecessary animations;
- no excessive cards;
- no generic SaaS aesthetic.

The design should feel personal rather than like a template.

---

## Important Principle

When modifying this website, preserve the underlying identity.

Before adding a section, feature or piece of content, ask:

> Does this help someone understand what kind of person Lorenzo is?

If not, it probably does not belong on the website.

The goal is not to show everything Lorenzo knows.

The goal is to make the visitor think:

> "This guy is curious, technically capable, understands business,
> and actually tries things."
